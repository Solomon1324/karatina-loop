KARATINA LOOP — CHAT, PHOTO AND CONTENT CONTROLS UPDATE

- Uploaded photos in group posts, comments, messages, business listings and campus information fit inside their display frames without stretching. The full photo is preserved with contain sizing.
- Live group chat supports public replies linked to a specific earlier message, and shows the referenced message above the reply.
- Approved group members can open private replies linked to a specific public chat message. Private rows use Supabase row-level security so only the sender and recipient can read them. Both users need approved group membership in the same approved group.
- Users can delete their own group posts, comments and chat messages. Group admins and site admins can moderate group content under database policies.
- Users can remove photos from their own text-containing posts, comments and messages while retaining the text. Photo-only items should be deleted rather than left empty.
- Users can delete their own business/campus comments, remove photos from their own submitted listing/information card, and remove photos from their own comments while retaining text. Site admins can moderate where allowed by database policies.
- Composer areas show a Remove stickers option whenever stickers are present in the draft. This strips sticker tokens while keeping the rest of the draft text. For stickers in already-published group content, use the available delete controls to remove the containing item.
- Live group chat listens for row updates/deletes, so open group dialogs refresh when content is removed.
